Data and comparison

The mouse view contains 9,240 measured neurons from two specimens. Twelve marker-enriched types provide the starting points for the cross-species comparison. Marmoset candidates were selected by comparing RNA profiles; the type selector follows each primary candidate match.

The official-coordinate view shows cells with available atlas coordinates, including 1,412 cells in the preoptic region. The reconstruction retains 4,759 candidate cells across 47 sections, with seven local components and six registration breaks marked in red. The two views cover different subsets of the atlas.

Coordinates and expression values come from my saved analysis. Marker colors show raw detected counts within each dataset. Sources: Allen Brain Cell Atlas · Marmoset Single Cell Atlas.
