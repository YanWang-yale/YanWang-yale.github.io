What is specified, and what is still a design choice?

This model follows the equipment list in my September 2026 application and implementation plan for the Unitree Robotics Young Talent Sponsorship Program. The application is under review. The immediate project is a monitoring system and an empty-cage test setup; closed-loop control belongs to a later stage.

I used the model to work through component placement, cable routing, and access to the cameras. It is not a drawing of an assembled or calibrated instrument.

How to read the optical overlay

The colored outlines show ideal geometric fields projected onto the nominal cage floor. They are calculated from the camera angles and the modeled lens height. They do not account for the wire grid, lens distortion, target height, focus, alignment error, or calibration. No transparent plate covers the thermal camera's line of sight.

What the replay means

All four signals are scripted examples. The surface-temperature trace is not core temperature; the activity index uses an arbitrary relative scale. The phase labels follow the replay timeline and are not predictions from a trained or validated classifier. Obstruction or stream loss changes the display to UNKNOWN and withholds the affected values.

The rig would record and flag changes. It does not control a heater, chiller, ultrasound device, or an animal's state. Independent core-temperature and metabolic measurements would be needed to evaluate any biological interpretation.

Model files and reproducibility

The current geometry was generated in Blender 5.2 LTS in metres and exported as glTF 2.0. I refined the frame slots, adjustment knobs, screw sockets, and wire-lid connections. These small mounting details are still design estimates. The browser uses a locally hosted, pinned Three.js release, with named assemblies for component inspection and reversible exploded views.
